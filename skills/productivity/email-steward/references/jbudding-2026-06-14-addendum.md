# jbudding@gmail.com — one occasion addendum

Condensed learning from the one occasion Email Steward runs. Use with the main jbudding heuristics and `references/jbudding-sender-patterns.md`.

## Flag / keep visible

- Current community or neighborhood hack/security updates (example: Encore Briar Chapel "Update on Hack") should be flagged while current. They can affect whether the user should trust messages from that community/contact.
- Instagram `security@mail.instagram.com` new-login alerts should be flagged while current; treat social account access notices as security-relevant even outside the hard Google/1Password/banking rules.
- Tumblr password-change/password notices from `no-reply@tumblr.com` should be flagged or kept visible as account-change/security events, even when old.
- Insurance injury-claim correspondence from a real insurer/person should be flagged or kept visible; it may have legal/financial implications.
- Google security alerts from `no-reply@accounts.google.com` may surface as current-window items even during old-backlog cleanup; flag all of them and group duplicates in the user-facing summary as related security threads.
- CanvasPrints, Fidelity-Life, TruGreen, PayPal, or similar brand/display-name lookalikes from unrelated personal/free senders with receipt/welcome/confirmation subjects should be flagged as phishing/spoof-pattern items, not deleted as ordinary promo debris.

## Skip / review

- Unknown real-person/service context should stay visible on first pass: examples from this run included a riding/exam notice, a home-service quote thread, a property/address email, and a sponsorship/team ask.
- Tinka/foreign-language billing, payment-term, or refund/credit notices should stay reviewed/visible on first pass. Treat unfamiliar foreign financial/account context conservatively unless a sender pattern is confirmed.
- Real-person Sabina Mora threads with comps/comparisons/property or travel/personal context should stay reviewed/visible; do not auto-archive based on age alone.

## Archive

- Wehkamp return confirmations and return-status notices are archive-safe as searchable purchase history once stale, unless they indicate an unresolved dispute.
- sweetgreen completed/received order records and Home Depot e-receipts are archive-safe as searchable purchase history.
- Wimberley Watershed / Wimberley Valley Watershed Association newsletters (`robingary@wimberleywatershed.org`) are archive-safe as searchable local/community newsletter history once stale and not an active civic/legal/action notice.
- De Telegraaf delivery-delay notices from `service@mail.telegraaf.nl` remain archive-safe as searchable account/service history once stale.

## Delete

- Thorlos retail promos, Twitch live notifications/offers, UT Austin CPE course promos, public Eventbrite/recruiting invites, Home Depot product-review prompts, Ticketmaster event newsletters, Sport Clips promos, At Home promos, Instagram social recommendations, and stale political/newsletter updates are delete-safe when old and not tied to orders, account/security, personal invitations, or receipts.
- Repeated legacy Dermstore, Thorlos, Havenly, Ticketmaster.nl event-newsletter, Instagram social, Dunham's Sports, UT CPE, Bob's Red Mill, and VirtualClinic newsletter/promo clusters are delete-safe when not tied to orders, account/security, personal invitations, receipts, or clinical documents.
- VROUW Glossy magazine/subscription promos, UPS My Choice feature promos, generic Poshmark liked-listing offers, and stale restaurant seasonal newsletters are delete-safe when they are not receipts, reservations, account/security notices, or personal correspondence.

# jbudding@gmail.com — one occasion addendum

Condensed learning from the one occasion Email Steward runs. Use with the main jbudding heuristics and `references/jbudding-sender-patterns.md`.

## Flag / keep visible

- Current Dolls Kill return-shipping reminders should be flagged and left visible while the return window may still require action. Stale Dolls Kill order/shipping records remain archive-safe as purchase history, and Dolls Kill retail/cart/promotional blasts remain delete-safe once stale.

## Skip / review

- Current BuildingLink / W Residences board, resident, or survey notices should stay reviewed/visible like other current W Residences notices. They may require human judgment even when they are not urgent operations or package alerts.
- Rue La La account/support threads and password-reset/account-access subjects should stay reviewed/visible on first pass. Treat them as account/security-adjacent rather than ordinary retail promo debris.
- Game-account ID/account notices such as Supercell ID should stay reviewed/visible on first pass when the subject suggests account setup or access context, even if old.
- Unfamiliar investment/update subjects from individual senders should stay reviewed/visible unless a clear promo-blast pattern is established; they may overlap business or finance context.
- Old bill-payment-scheduled notices should stay reviewed/visible conservatively when they involve real financial accounts.

## Archive

- Austin Water / MyATXWater conservation newsletters and non-urgent utility/local-service announcements can be archived as searchable utility/local-service history when they are not billing warnings, outage notices, shutoff notices, or action-required account mail.

## Delete

- Legacy Dermstore, Havenly, Dunham's Sports, Ticketmaster event-newsletter, Instagram social, UT Austin CPE course/program, Poshmark liked-listing, 365Chess course-sale, and stale restaurant seasonal promo clusters remain delete-safe when they are not tied to orders, account/security, receipts, personal invitations, or support threads.
